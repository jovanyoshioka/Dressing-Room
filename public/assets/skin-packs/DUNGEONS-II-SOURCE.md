# Dungeons II Hero skin textures

Original Minecraft skin artwork: Mojang / Microsoft.

The 21 unchanged 64×64 PNG textures and their slim-model designations were obtained from the Dungeons II Hero section of [Modrinth’s bundled Minecraft skin catalog](https://github.com/modrinth/code/blob/main/packages/app-lib/src/api/minecraft_skins/assets/default/default_skins.rs). Source-file SHA-256 is recorded in `SOURCE.txt` inside `dungeons_ii_hero.mcpack`.

Included: Darian, Edson, Eshe, Esperanza, Flores, Gaston, Greta, Gubbis, Javier, Kellen, Mats, Nuru, Pim, Qamar, Raven, Ren, Riggs, Stella, Tula, Valorie, Violet.

The importable pack wrapper, localization, and app integration were assembled for this project; this is not an official Marketplace download package. Attribution does not change the original artwork’s ownership or grant additional rights.
